FloodGuard Support
Find answers or send a message to the project support team.
How does FloodGuard calculate risk?+
FloodGuard combines available monitoring signals such as rainfall, water level, terrain and camera evidence to present a risk estimate.
Why is my dashboard showing a warning?+
Warnings appear when monitored evidence crosses the configured risk thresholds or when an important data-quality issue is detected.
How do I change my alert preferences?+
Open Settings from the sidebar, change your monitoring and notification preferences, and select Save settings.
How do I log out?+
Use the Logout button at the bottom of the left sidebar. Your current session will be securely cleared.
